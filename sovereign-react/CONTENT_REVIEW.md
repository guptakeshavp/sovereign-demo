## Still labelled as demonstration data in the UI
Prices and surcharges; cloth names, hand and weight ("placeholder spec"); garment and body size charts ("indicative demo figures, pending sampling"); delivery, returns and payment methods ("await client approval"); product names (working names).

## Content Sovereign needs to approve before launch
1. Place of manufacture and any "made in" statement.
2. Whether garments are made to order, and lead times.
3. Whether, where and how the cross stitch appears on garments.
4. Made-to-measure process: fittings (in person / video / self-measure), what is included, the fee.
5. Alterations and returns policy, especially for customised pieces.
6. Cloth list, compositions and sources.
7. Construction details (lining, buttons, canvas) and any craftsmanship language.
8. Final product names and prices.
9. Studio location and appointment model.
