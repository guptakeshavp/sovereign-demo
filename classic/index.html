How a Sovereign is made

The cloth comes first.

Worsted wools and wool-silk blends from mills we have visited and would visit again. Each cloth is chosen for how it drapes on the second wear, not the first.

A canvas gives it shape.

A floating half-canvas is sewn in by hand so the chest rolls with you rather than sitting flat. It is why a Sovereign looks better in a year than it does today.

The cut is yours alone.

Your measurements, your posture, your preferences. A pattern is drafted for you and kept on file, so the second commission is simpler than the first.

One copper cross stitch.

Where the lioness wears hers, every piece carries a single stitch on the inside. Small enough that only you know it is there. That is the point.
